Experiment Methodology

Standardized 10-step protocol for clinical workflow evaluation

  1. Select a de-identified sample case: Choose from standardized de-identified registry cases (CASE-001 through CASE-005).
  2. Identify the required evidence categories: Review required diagnostic domains (Pathology, Imaging, Molecular, Specimen Lineage, Review Decision).
  3. Measure baseline timeline assembly time: Use the Baseline Stopwatch to measure the duration required to manually access disparate reports and draft the timeline.
  4. Measure prototype timeline assembly time: Use the Prototype Stopwatch to measure the duration required to open and review the unified multidisciplinary timeline.
  5. Compare both measurements: Directly contrast the elapsed duration of both approaches on the same case.
  6. Calculate time saved: Compute the exact difference: Time Saved = Baseline Time − Prototype Time.
  7. Calculate percentage improvement: Compute efficiency gain: Improvement % = ((Baseline − Prototype) / Baseline) × 100.
  8. Record errors or delays: Use the Error Analysis form to log causes of friction (e.g. missing molecular panel or stale imaging).
  9. Repeat for multiple cases: Execute the standardized measurement across all 5 test cases to ensure statistical validity.
  10. Calculate average results: Aggregate the mean baseline duration, mean prototype duration, mean time reduction, and target compliance.

Project Summary

Core architectural essence of the multidisciplinary pathology evidence system
Problem:

Difficulty assembling multidisciplinary pathology, imaging, molecular, specimen, and review evidence into one reliable timeline.

Solution:

A web-based evidence timeline prototype that organizes multidisciplinary case information and provides freshness indicators, role-based views, specimen lineage, scheduling, MDT review, failure handling, and experiment measurement.

Technology:

HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JavaScript + Git/GitHub (Zero external framework dependencies).

Safety:

Missing/stale/unreliable evidence triggers warnings and manual expert-review fallback.

Evaluation:

Failure-mode testing and timeline-assembly time measurement are included.
